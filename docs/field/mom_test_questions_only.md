# Arda Link — Maswali ya Mom Test kwa Wachungaji

*Kiswahili (kuuliza) + English (reference). Dakika 40–60. Sehemu 13, maswali 60.*

---

## Kabla ya kuanza

**Sema hivi:** Habari. Jina langu ni ___, natoka Arda Link. Tunatengeneza huduma ya simu
kwa wachungaji, na tunataka kujua matatizo yenu halisi. Hili ni mahojiano, si mradi wa
msaada, na **hakuna malipo ya fedha**. Huenda tusitengeneze kitu kabisa. Nitakuuliza
maswali kama dakika 45. Naomba kuandika na kurekodi, ili nisikose ulichosema. Unaweza
kusimama, au kuruka swali lolote, wakati wowote. Jina lako halitatumika popote bila
ruhusa yako. Tukianza?

**Sheria nne (usizivunje)**

1. **Uliza jinsi alivyofanya, si unavyofikiri.** *(Ask what he did, not what he thinks.)*
2. **Uliza mara ya mwisho, si wakati ujao.** "Ungefanya nini?" si jibu. "Ulifanya nini
   mara ya mwisho?" ni jibu. *(Past tense only. Never "would you…".)*
3. **Omba namba kila wakati.** Siku ngapi? Kilometa ngapi? Shilingi ngapi? Wanyama
   wangapi? Lita ngapi? Saa ngapi? *(Numbers or it did not happen.)*
4. **Usimwambie tunatengeneza nini.** Ukizungumza kuhusu bidhaa yetu zaidi ya sekunde 30,
   hujajifunza kitu. *(Never pitch. If he asks: one line, then back to his last season.)*

**Wacha kufanya hivi:** kusema "ni wazo zuri", kukubali kila kitu, kuuliza "ni rahisi?"
*(Do not accept compliments as data. Do not guide the phone. Do not fill his silences.)*

---

## A. Maisha ya kawaida · A normal day

1. Niambie jana — kutoka ulipoamka hadi ulipolala. Ulifanya nini? *(Tell me about
   yesterday — wake-up to bed. What did you do?)*
2. Nani alikuwa na mifugo jana? Walienda wapi? *(Who was with the animals yesterday, and
   where did they go?)*
3. Nani mwingine anaishi manyatta yako, na kila mmoja anafanya kazi gani? *(Who else lives
   here, and who does what?)*

## B. Uamuzi wa mwisho mgumu · The last hard decision

4. Mara ya mwisho ulipohamisha mifugo, ilikuwa mwezi gani? Nini kilikufanya uamue? *(When
   did you last move the animals — which month — and what made you decide?)*
5. Uliuliza nani? Kila mmoja alisema nini? *(Who did you ask, and what did each say?)*
6. Ulichukua siku ngapi kuamua — kuanzia ulipofikiria hadi siku ya kuhama? *(How many days
   from first thought to moving?)*
7. Ulijaribu nini kwanza, kabla ya kuhama? *(What did you try first, before moving?)*
8. Kuhama kulikugharimu nini — fedha, mifugo, siku, uhusiano? *(What did the move cost you
   in money, animals, days, relationships?)*
9. Kulikuwa na wakati ulikaribia kwenda mahali pengine? Kwa nini hukwenda? *(Was there a
   moment you nearly went somewhere else — why not?)*

## C. Maji · Water

10. Mara ya mwisho ulipofika maji na maji hayakuwa ya kutosha — chanzo gani, mwezi gani,
    ulitembea muda gani? *(The last time you walked to water and it was not enough —
    which point, which month, how long did you walk?)*
11. Ulifanya nini siku hiyo? Ulienda mahali pengine? Umbali gani? *(What did you do that
    day — did you go elsewhere, how far?)*
12. Siku hiyo ilikugharimu nini — saa, fedha, mafuta, mifugo, hasira? *(What did that day
    cost you — hours, money, fuel, animals, tempers?)*
13. Kuna chanzo uliacha kwenda? Kwa nini? *(Is there a point you stopped going to — why?)*
14. Nani anakuambia kama maji yapo, au foleni ni ndefu? Unajuaje? *(Who tells you whether
    the water is working, or the queue is long? How do you find out?)*
15. Uliwahi kulipa mtu kwa taarifa ya maji? Nini kilitokea? *(Have you ever paid someone
    for information about water — what happened?)*

## D. Malisho · Grazing and pasture

16. Malisho karibu na maji yako yalikwisha lini? Mwezi gani? *(When did the grass near
    your water last run out — which month?)*
17. Ulijuaje yamekwisha — nani aliona, na aliona nini? *(How did you know it was finished —
    who saw it, and what did they see?)*
18. Ulifanya nini baada ya hapo? Nani alikushauri? Ilifanikiwa? *(What did you do next, who
    suggested it, did it work?)*
19. Ulipoteza mifugo mingapi, au maziwa kiasi gani, katika kipindi hicho? *(How many animals
    or how much milk did you lose in that period?)*
20. Kuna mtu anakuambia mapema mahali malisho yapo? Nani, mara ngapi? *(Does anyone tell
    you in advance where the grass is good — who, how often?)*
21. Uliwahi kulipa kwa taarifa ya malisho — simu, usafiri kwenda kuangalia, zawadi? *(Have
    you ever paid for news about pasture — a call, transport to go and look, a gift?)*

## E. Kupe, minyoo na afya · Ticks, worms, animal health

22. Mara ya mwisho ulipoona kupe au minyoo, ilikuwa mwezi gani? *(When did you last find
    ticks or worms — which month?)*
23. Ulifanya nini kwanza? Uliuliza nani? *(What did you do first, and who did you ask?)*
24. Ulinunua nini, kwa nani, bei gani? Ilisaidia? *(What did you buy, from whom, at what
    price — did it work?)*
25. Nani alikuambia uangalie mifugo — au uliona mwenyewe? *(Who told you to look at the
    animals — or did you just see it?)*
26. Kama ungeambiwa mapema kwamba hali ya kupe inaandaa, ungefanya nini tofauti?
    *(If you had been told earlier that tick conditions were forming, what would you have
    done differently — what did you do last time?)*

## F. Habari za sasa · Where information comes from today

27. Habari za mvua na malisho unazipata wapi? Redio, chief, majirani, kikundi cha WhatsApp,
    daktari wa mifugo? Kipi kwanza? *(Where do you get news of rain and grazing — radio,
    chief, neighbours, a WhatsApp group, the vet? Which first?)*
28. Kipi kimewahi kukosea na kikakugharimu? Nini kilitokea? *(Which of those has been wrong
    in a way that cost you — what happened?)*
29. Uliamini nini wiki iliyopita ambacho hakikuwa kweli? Uligundua vipi? *(What did you
    believe last week that turned out to be wrong — how did you find out?)*
30. Mwezi wa kawaida unatumia kiasi gani kwa airtime na bundle? *(What do you spend a normal
    month on airtime and data?)*
31. Uko kwenye kikundi cha WhatsApp cha wachungaji? Wangapi? Kinaitwaje? Nani anasambaza kwa
    nani? *(Are you in a herders' WhatsApp group — how many, what is it called, who forwards
    to whom?)*

## G. Simu yenyewe · The phone itself

32. Simu hii ni ya nani? SIM ngapi? Nani mwingine anaitumia? *(Whose phone is this, how many
    SIMs, who else uses it?)*
33. Mara ya mwisho hamna mtandao malishoni, ilikuwa lini? Kwa muda gani? *(When did you last
    have no network where you keep animals — for how long?)*
34. Unachaji wapi? Betri ikikwisha, unafanya nini? *(Where do you charge it — what happens
    when the battery dies?)*
35. Unasoma kwa raha? Unasoma ujumbe, au unasikiliza sauti? *(Can you read comfortably — do
    you read messages or listen to voice notes?)*
36. Nionyeshe unafanya nini ukipata ujumbe usioelewa. *(Show me what you do when you get a
    message you do not understand — I will just watch.)*

## H. Fedha · Money

37. Mwezi uliopita ulitumia kiasi gani kwa mifugo — dawa, maji, usafiri, chakula, vet?
    *(Last month, what did you spend on the animals — drugs, water, transport, feed, a vet?)*
38. Ukiitaji shilingi 5,000 haraka, unauza nini, au unampigia nani? *(If you needed 5,000
    shillings quickly, what would you sell or who would you call?)*
39. Uliwahi kununua bundle, huduma ya SMS, au kubeti? Kiasi gani kwa mwezi? *(Have you ever
    paid for a bundle, an SMS service or betting — how much a month?)*
40. Uliwahi kulipa kwa ushauri — vet, agrovet, broker, mtu wa usafiri? Bei gani? Ilifaa?
    *(Have you ever paid for advice — vet, agrovet, broker, transporter? How much, and was it
    worth it?)*

## I. Kuamini na kukosewa · Trust and being wrong

41. Niambie wakati ushauri kuhusu mifugo au mvua ulikosea. Ulifanya nini? *(Tell me about a
    time advice on animals or rain was wrong — what did you do?)*
42. Simu ikikuambia mvua inakuja siku tano, kisha isinyeshe — utaifanyia nini simu hiyo?
    *(If a phone said rain was coming in five days and it did not come, what would you do
    with that phone?)*
43. Kuhusu mvua, unamwamini nani: chief, redio, vet, jirani, au ujumbe wa simu? Kwa nini?
    *(Who do you believe about rain — chief, radio, vet, neighbour or a phone message? Why?)*
44. Nini kitakufanya uache kuamini huduma kama hii kabisa? *(What would make you stop
    trusting a service like this completely?)*
45. Huduma ikisema "hatujui", ni bora au mbaya kuliko kubahatisha? *(If a service says "we do
    not know", is that better or worse than a guess?)*

## J. Wengine walijaribu · What others have tried

46. Mashirika gani yamewahi kuwapa taarifa au huduma kama hizi — NGO, county, redio, kanisa,
    bima? Nini kilitokea? Inaendelea? *(Which organisations have given you information or
    services like this — NGO, county, radio, church, insurance? What happened, is it still
    running?)*
47. Kwa nini kiliisha — au nini kingekufanya uendelee kuitumia? *(Why did it stop — or what
    would have made you keep using it?)*

## K. Nyumbani · Household and beneficiary

48. Unapopata taarifa mapema, nani ananufaika kwanza nyumbani, na anapata nini? *(When you
    get news early, who in the household benefits first, and what do they get?)*
49. Nani anachota maji na kunyunyizia mbuzi? Kutembea kunabadilikaje kwa misimu? *(Who
    fetches water and waters the small stock — how does the walking change with the season?)*
50. Nani anaamua kuhusu mbuzi na kondoo, na fedha za maziwa? *(Who decides about the shoats,
    and the milk money?)*
51. Nyumbani kwako kungebadilika nini kama hutapoteza safari hata moja kwa maji mwaka huu?
    *(What would change in your house if you never wasted a single walk for water this
    year?)*

## L. Kuchangia taarifa · Giving information

52. Uliwahi kuambia mtu mwingine kwamba maji yameharibika, au yanafanya kazi? Ulifanyaje —
    simu, kikundi, kwa mdomo? *(Have you ever told anyone that a water point was broken or
    working — how: a call, the group, in person?)*
53. Mara ya mwisho ulipokuwa maji, ungeweza kutuma ujumbe mmoja kama ungeombwa? Ulifanya
    hivyo? *(The last time you were at the water, could you have sent one message if you had
    been asked — did you?)*
54. Unapopeana taarifa kwa kikundi, hufanyaje ili watu waamini — picha, sauti, jina lako?
    *(When you pass information to the group, how do you make people believe it — a photo, a
    voice note, your name?)*
55. Nini kingekufanya uache kuchangia taarifa? *(What would make you stop contributing
    information?)*

## M. Omba kitu · Close: ask for something

56. Naweza kurudi au kukupigia baada ya wiki mbili? *(May I come back or call you in two
    weeks? — a time commitment)*
57. Unaweza kunionyesha simu yako, nitume ujumbe mmoja sasa, nawe ukiangalia? *(Would you
    show me your phone and let me send one message now, while you watch?)*
58. Ni nani mwingine ninayepaswa kuongea naye — na **nani hatakubaliana** nawe? *(Who else
    should I talk to — and who would disagree with you?)*
59. Tunaweza kufanya jaribio kwenye chanzo chako cha maji, pamoja na kamati? *(Could we run a
    test at your water point, with the committee?)*
60. Nimekosea nini leo? Nilipaswa kuuliza nini, na sikuuliza? *(What did I get wrong today —
    what should I have asked and did not?)*

---

## Baada ya mahojiano (dakika 20) · After the interview (20 minutes)

1. **Andika hadithi ya sentensi tatu:** yeye ni nani, tatizo moja lililokuwa muhimu zaidi,
   na anafanya nini leo. *(Three sentences: who he is, the one problem that mattered most,
   what he does about it today.)*
2. **Orodhesha namba zote ulizoandika.** Chini ya tatu = mahojiano yalikuwa ya heshima
   kupita kiasi. *(List every number. Fewer than three means the interview was too polite.)*
3. **Andika kitu kimoja tunapaswa kuacha kuamini.** *(One thing we should stop believing.)*
4. **Weka alama kwa kila taarifa:** FACT · NUMBER · QUOTE · WORKAROUND · PAYMENT ·
   TRUST-BREAK · COMMITMENT · BLOCKER. *(Tag every note.)*

**Ukikumbuka kitu kimoja tu:** *alifanya nini mara ya mwisho?* — na omba namba.
*(If you remember one thing: what did he do last time — and ask for the number.)*
