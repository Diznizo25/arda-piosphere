# Arda Link — the loss ledger

*What we protect, how we protect it, and what we may never claim.*

## The mission in one line

**Protect what he has, grow it, sell it well, and unlock what it's worth.**

Everything we build is one of four moves: stop a loss, add value to an animal he
already owns, get a better price for it, or turn his record into something others will
pay for (insurance, credit, buyers). If a feature isn't one of those four, it isn't
priority.

## Why the mission alone isn't retention

A mission is the *why*; habits come from a loop. Loss reduction only keeps a herder
coming back when it is:

- **visible** — he can see the losses he did *not* suffer ("mwaka huu umepoteza 3, si 8"),
- **attributable** — the cause is connected to something he did ("ulihamisha kabla ya
  bwawa kukauka", "ulikamua kupe mvua ilipoanza"),
- **recurring** — a weekly rhythm, not a one-off rescue,
- **reciprocal** — his one tap gives him the ward's answer, not just our advice.

## Every cause of loss, and what we can honestly do

| Cause | How often | Money at stake | Our lever | What we may claim | What we may never claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Drought — water** | seasonal, certain | animals, and whole herds in a bad year | water status network, runway estimate, where to move | "your pan may dry in N days, here is why, here is who is at the next one" | an exact date; that a point will *certainly* be dry |
| **Drought — pasture** | seasonal | weight, milk, fertility | pasture maps, green-up front, ranked directions | "the grass is greening 40 km north" (with the snapshot's date) | that a patch will still be good when he arrives |
| **Parasites (ticks, worms)** | every wet season | 5–20% of production | pest windows, check-first loop, tick index from reports | "conditions favour ticks this week, look here on the animal" | that his animals *have* anything |
| **Disease** | episodic | one animal to a herd | triage to a human, case card, pooled vet day | "these signs need a vet today; here is the number and the case" | a diagnosis, a drug, a dose, "it's nothing serious" |
| **Rustling / theft** | episodic, costly | one or many animals | marks registry, animal card, loss relay rings | "this animal is missing, here are its marks and prints" | that a specific person or clan took it |
| **Predators** | episodic (night) | calves, shoats | manyatta-level night alerts | "a predator was seen near X last night" | a guarantee of protection |
| **Straying / mixing** | common, cheap to fix | days of searching | evening roll-call + relays | "one is missing from the evening count" | where it is |
| **Pan/dam accidents, floods** | episodic | many animals at once | wet-season warnings tied to heavy-rain forecasts | "heavy rain is coming; pans overflow" | that it will happen at his spot |
| **Selling badly** | 2–6 sales a year, every year | **10–30% of the price** | grade card, price bands from the poll, sell/hold call | the ward's band for that grade, with the sample size | a single "correct" price |
| **Selling thin** | every dry season | 10–25% of value | sample weighing, condition trend, fattening calendar | his own measurements, tracked | kilos/condition from an unvalidated model (gated until shadow-validated) |
| **Keeping unproductive stock** | quietly, every year | feed and attention | herd composition + offtake advice | "this animal has produced nothing in 2 years" (from *his* records) | forcing a cull |
| **Treating late / twice** | common | the cost of the animal, twice | episode tracking, reminders, withdrawal card | "day 3 of the treatment is today" | what to give |
| **Milk and calf loss** | seasonal | daily income, future herd | milk-drop watch (where milking exists), calf checks | "her milk dropped 6 → 2" | why |
| **Market fraud / short payment** | episodic | the whole price | price bands, weight evidence, buyer bids | what the ward got this week | that a named trader is dishonest |
| **Movement permits / fines** | episodic | a day and a fee | paperwork prep with the vet's office, market-day reminders | "you need the permit before you move them" | anything about the county's enforcement |
| **Conflict / road insecurity** | episodic | animals and lives | manyatta-level area warnings, no names | "avoid this route today" | blame of any group |
| **Feed and transport waste** | continuous | small but constant | pooled transport, group input buying | the shared cost per household | that it is always cheaper |

## Honesty rules

1. **A loss we cannot reduce, we do not pretend to reduce.** Drought scale, conflict,
   and floods beyond our warning are named plainly when they happen.
2. **Counts and events he reported are ground truth.** Estimates (runway, weights,
   values, condition) are labelled as estimates and gated until shadow-validated.
3. **No false alarm.** A warning that costs him money or a wasted trip is a loss we
   caused — hence the check-first wording and the conservative tiers.
4. **Never a diagnosis, never a drug, never a promise of a vet, never an accusation.**
5. **Every claim traceable to his own data or a neighbour's report** — and say which.

## What "mentor" means, concretely

A mentor in pastoralist terms is an elder who knows the herds, the places, the market
and the seasons; who is honest; who remembers; who is available; who introduces you to
the right people; and who quietly keeps score. Mapped onto the product:

| Mentor behaviour | What we build |
|---|---|
| **Remembers** | his manyatta, his animals, his point, his last reports, his last sale — never asking twice |
| **Watches** | the evening count, the water status at his point, the pest window, the rain for his place |
| **Warns early and rarely** | onset, dry spell, water drying, loss, predators — never a daily stream of noise |
| **Answers his questions** | his own question, his own language, voice in and voice out, grounded in his facts |
| **Introduces** | the vet, the agrovet, the buyer, the transporter, the ward's pooled days |
| **Keeps score, quietly** | the yearly line: losses, gains, price achieved, herd change |
| **Never lectures, never shames** | no hygiene sermons; money framing instead; his data stays his |
| **Admits uncertainty** | "hatuna data ya kutosha" is a valid answer |

An exchange looks like this — his question, his facts, options instead of orders:

```
Herder: nimuue ng'ombe wangu au nisubiri?

Arda:   Ng'ombe wako: maziwa lita 4 siku hii (uliripoti).
        Bei ya eneo wiki hii: 55,000–65,000 (mauzo 11, Oldonyiro).
        Mvua: siku 8–15 zijazo ishara zinaanza.

        Ukiuza sasa: pesa haraka, lakini kwa bei ya kati.
        Ukisubiri: malisho yanaimarika, lakini mvua ikikosa unapoteza
        kilo. Uamuzi ni wako. Nikuonyeshe wanunuzi?

Herder: ndio

Arda:   Wanunuzi 2 wa eneo lako wanataka ng'ombe mzima sasa.
        Tuma '1' nikufungulie mawasiliano.
```

## The scoreboard — the thing that makes them stay

Once a month, and once a year, one card built **only from what he reported**:

```
📊 MWAKA WAKO — Oldonyiro

Wanyama waliopotea: 3   (mwaka jana: 8)
  waliopatikana: 2      (kwa mtandao wa vyanzo jirani)
Maji: ulihamisha mara 2 kabla ya bwawa kukauka
Kupe: ulijibu tahadhari 5 kati ya 6
Bei uliyopata: wastani 61,000 kwa ng'ombe mzima
  (wastani wa eneo: 57,500)
Kundi: 58 → 64

Ushindi wa mwaka: ulipoteza wanyama 5 wachache, na uliuza juu kwa 6%.
```

That is the retention engine inside the mission: it is **his** score, in **his** numbers,
from **his** reports — and it proves the promise quietly, every month, without a single
unvalidated estimate.

## Build order (what makes the score possible)

1. **The record** — evening roll-call + herd register (cohort counts + exception events),
   so losses, gains and changes are counted, not guessed.
2. **The channel** — water-point and manyatta relays, so a loss is answered by a network
   and the recovery is a number we can report.
3. **The poll** — one-tap ward answers, which supply the price bands, tick index, water
   status and pasture reports that the score and the warnings are built from.
4. **The mentor surfaces** — his-history line, his-hour delivery, relevance filter,
   preferences and opt-outs.
5. **The scoreboard** — monthly and yearly, from reports only.
6. **Gated, shadow-validated layers** — weights, values, condition scoring, photo
   grading: computed in shadow, published only after they clear the accuracy gate.
7. **The money rails** — price bands, buyer bids, pooled days, grader card; then the
   institutional lines (insurance verification, county contracts, aggregation).

## The two numbers that prove the mission

- **Losses per household per year** (animals died, rustled, strayed, sold badly) — going
  down.
- **Realised sale price** versus the ward band — going up.

If those two move, the system is doing its job. If they don't, no amount of features
will matter.
